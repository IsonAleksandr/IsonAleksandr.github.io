Cinematic horror, made with AI.

Ison is a small creative studio making cinematic horror and apocalyptic short films, two to five minutes long, built with AI tools and directed by hand.

The current series, That Day and Hour, follows ordinary people through a catastrophe of biblical proportions, where the dead come back mutated and hungry.

New episodes land on YouTube and X, with trailers on Instagram. Want them first? Join Ison Insiders.
